PIAW - prototype v0.1
"Piaw!" - the asafo battle cry
Play: open Godot > Import > choose this folder's project.godot > press Play (F5)

Controls          Keyboard/mouse      PS5 controller
Move / look       WASD / mouse        Left / right stick
Fire Dane gun     Left click          R2
Aim               Q                   L2
Afena slash       F / right click     R1
Crouch (hold)     C                   Circle
Reload            R                   Square
Sprint / jump     Shift / Space       L3 / Cross

Stealth: crouch inside bushes = HIDDEN. Afena on an unaware enemy = instant kill.
Mission: ambush the column (5), then take the hill fort (4 + cannon), stand at the flag.
